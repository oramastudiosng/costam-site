Why this exists

My wife is learning to bake. She went for classes, and came home from one of them properly frustrated.

It was not the mixing or the icing. It was the costing.

Because here is the part nobody warns you about. Flour comes in a bag. Sugar comes in mudu. Butter comes in a tub. Then the recipe asks for 300 grams of this and two of that. Working out what one cake really costs you means doing that conversion for every single ingredient — and doing it again every time a price moves, which in this country is every month.

So most bakers do not do it. They price by feel, or by whatever the woman down the road is charging. And they honestly do not know whether a particular cake made them money or quietly cost them money. Many find out much later, if at all.

That is the one thing this app is for. You tell it what you paid and how you bought it. It tells you what one cake costs you to make.

It does not tell you what to charge. That is your decision and you know your customers. It only makes sure the number you are deciding from is a real one.

  • Buy the way you actually buy

    Bags, mudu, derica, crates, cartons, litres. Not just grams.

  • Change one price, everything updates

    Flour went up? Change it once. Every recipe that uses it follows.

  • Works with no internet at all

    Everything lives on your phone. Airplane mode, no network, no problem.

Installing it

CostAm is not on the Play Store yet, so your phone will ask you to confirm before it installs. This is normal for an app you install directly, and the phone is right to ask.

Here is what you will see.

  1. Tap the download button. The file is about 106 MB. Use wifi if you have it.
  2. Your browser may warn you about the file type. Tap Download anyway. It is an app file, which is what you asked for.
  3. Open the file from your notifications, or find it in your Downloads.
  4. Android will say something like “your phone is not allowed to install unknown apps from this source”. Tap Settings, turn on Allow from this source, then press back.

    Different phones word this differently — Tecno, Infinix, Samsung and Redmi each have their own version. Whatever it says, you are looking for a switch that lets this one app install. It is Android checking you meant to do this, and you only do it once.

  5. Tap Install. Then Open.

That is it. The app opens with some common ingredients already in it, at zero price. Put in what you actually pay, and it starts working.

Where your prices go

Nowhere.

CostAm has no account, no login, and no internet connection. Your ingredient prices and recipes are saved on your phone and nothing else. We cannot see them. Nobody can.

There is a Back up my data button in Settings that gives you a file. Keep it somewhere safe — WhatsApp it to yourself, or put it in Google Drive. That file is how you move everything to a new phone.
